Path to Apotheosis

Levels 13-20 for DnD 5.5e All-in-One BEYOND

Baldur's Gate 3 stops at level 12, and so does DnD 5.5e. Path to Apotheosis takes every class to level 20: the full D&D curve, following the 2024 Player's Handbook and built on top of DnD 5.5e rather than replacing it.

What it adds

  • Levels 13-20 for every class DnD 5.5e ships, including its Artificer, Gunslinger, Illrigger and Monster Hunter.
  • Subclass features 13-20, each at its 2024 level.
  • 7th-, 8th- and 9th-level spells and spell slots, on each class's rules spell list, learned through the normal level-up screen, plus upcasting to 9th level for existing spells.
  • Epic Boons at level 19: all 12 from the 2024 PHB, plus boons from Heroes of Faerun and Arcana Unleashed.
  • Pools and uses that keep growing: Hit Point Dice, Favored Enemy, Arcane Recovery, Healing Light, Balm, Arcane Ward, Natural Recovery, Psionic Energy Dice, Proficiency Bonus uses and more follow the 2024 tables to level 20.
  • Sangromancer (Grim Hollow wizard) and Sanguine Thief (Grim Hollow rogue) levels 13-20 (Sanguine Thief: third-caster slots, Bloodstitch at 13, Bloody Exit at 17), the Sangromancer with its five Sangromancy spells, Artificer level 14 plans and level 15 elixirs, and Circle of the Moon forms at the rules' Challenge Rating tiers (Elephant, Triceratops, Mammoth).
  • Portal Jumper, the Arcana Unleashed origin feat, in the Human origin feat pick at character creation.
  • Features back at their real levels: DnD 5.5e squeezes some level 13+ features into lower levels to fit the cap; Apotheosis moves them back to their 2024 levels. Apart from that, levels 1-12 play exactly as DnD 5.5e alone.

Requirements

  • DnD 5.5e All-in-One BEYOND 4.14.0.2 (also on Nexus Mods, mod 12727) - load it before Path to Apotheosis
  • BG3 Script Extender
  • Optional: Mod Configuration Menu for an in-game settings page (XP multiplier, Epic Boon level gate, Portal Jumper, debug logging)

Notes

Made for DnD 5.5e All-in-One BEYOND 4.14.0.2. Other mods that change the level cap, XP table or class progressions above 12 will conflict. Existing saves are not tested; a new game is recommended. Every class and subclass build is levelled 1 to 20 in the running game by an automated test harness before each release.
